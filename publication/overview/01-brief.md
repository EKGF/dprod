A practical guide for product owners, data leaders, architects, and the teams who publish and consume data.

## The idea in a minute

A data product needs more than a name and a download link. People need to understand its purpose, who owns it, how to access it, and which shared concepts give its data meaning.

The Data Product Ontology (DPROD) provides a shared vocabulary for those descriptions. It builds on the W3C Data Catalog Vocabulary (DCAT), using established Linked Data technologies instead of asking each organization to invent another metadata format.

> DPROD connects descriptions across teams. The data itself can stay where it belongs.

## What this guide covers

- Why shared descriptions matter in a decentralized data ecosystem.
- How products, ports, datasets, and distributions fit together.
- A worked example that connects a product to a shared financial schema.
- The optional role of data contracts and a practical adoption path.

This is an EKGF community overview, not a replacement for the normative specification. DPROD's published OMG baseline and the evolving community work are distinct publications; check the [specification versions](https://ekgf.org/dprod/spec-versions/) before selecting a version for implementation.
