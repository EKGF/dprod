## Adoption is a sequence of decisions

A small working description establishes a shared foundation. A broader rollout adds organizational conventions: stable identifiers, ownership responsibilities, publication locations, validation in CI, and a process for evolving descriptions.

The project's [roadmap](https://ekgf.org/dprod/roadmap/) and [repository](https://github.com/EKGF/dprod) are the source of current plans. This guide does not turn planned work into a claim of implemented capability.

## A practical rollout conversation

| Question | Decision to make |
| --- | --- |
| Who publishes the description? | Identify the product owner and publishing responsibility. |
| Which version do we use? | Select the baseline or development publication deliberately. |
| Where do identities live? | Agree durable product, service, and dataset identifiers. |
| How do we check changes? | Validate descriptions and review changes with their consumers. |
| What needs an extension? | Reuse existing vocabularies before introducing local terms. |
| When do contracts help? | Add explicit commitments when provider and consumer need them. |

## Work with the community

DPROD is developed through the EKGF community and the OMG standardization process. Teams can learn from the published examples, raise concrete implementation questions, and contribute proposed improvements through the project's normal review process.
