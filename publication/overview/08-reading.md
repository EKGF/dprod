## Choose the depth you need

**For a first conversation**

- [DPROD project website](https://ekgf.org/dprod/): purpose, concepts, adoption, and roadmap.
- [Core concepts](https://ekgf.org/dprod/concepts/): the main building blocks in plain language.

**For an implementation**

- [Specification versions](https://ekgf.org/dprod/spec-versions/): choose the publication appropriate for your work.
- [Worked examples](https://github.com/EKGF/dprod/tree/develop/examples): concrete product descriptions and optional contract patterns.
- [Source repository](https://github.com/EKGF/dprod): ontology, shapes, contexts, issues, and contribution history.

**For the underlying standards**

- [DCAT](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-dcat-3/): catalogs, datasets, distributions, and data services.
- [JSON-LD](https://www.w3.org/TR/json-ld11/): JSON documents with linked-data meaning.
- [SHACL](https://www.w3.org/TR/shacl/): validation of RDF descriptions.
- [ODRL](https://www.w3.org/TR/odrl-model/): machine-readable permissions, prohibitions, and duties.
