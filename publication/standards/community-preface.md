## About the Enterprise Knowledge Graph Forum

The [Enterprise Knowledge Graph Forum (EKGF)](https://ekgf.org/) is an OMG community in which initiatives such as DPROD are developed and reviewed before they are handed over for formal standardisation. The [Enterprise Knowledge Graph Platform Task Force](https://www.omg.org/ekg/) provides the OMG standards process for this work.

This community edition invites practical feedback on the data product vocabulary, the optional Data Contracts profile and their examples. It is not an approved OMG specification and does not assign formal publication status to the draft.

## Review and participation

Use the [DPROD issue tracker](https://github.com/EKGF/dprod/issues) to report technical issues and the [repository pull requests](https://github.com/EKGF/dprod/pulls) to propose improvements. The source repository is the shared basis for this community edition and the OMG submission preview; technical changes are made once and appear in both.
