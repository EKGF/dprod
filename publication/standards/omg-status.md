This is the **DPROD 1.1 OMG submission preview** prepared from the EKGF community source repository for review through the [OMG Enterprise Knowledge Graph Platform Task Force](https://www.omg.org/ekg/). It is not an approved OMG specification and does not assert that a formal submission has been accepted.

**OMG document number: not assigned for this preview.** No approval status, formal release date or official 1.1 publication URL is claimed. Those values must be supplied by the publisher before the separately configured formal release target can resolve.

The official baseline remains [DPROD 1.0 beta 2](https://www.omg.org/spec/DPROD/1.0/Beta2/PDF), OMG document ptc/26-05-40. This edition preserves the source legal preamble and includes the formal scope, conformance, references, terminology, symbols, namespaces, models and worked examples. The publisher preface and bibliographic editions carried over from that baseline remain subject to confirmation for 1.1.

The EKGF community draft and this submission preview share the same technical placements and source commit. The closing provenance section records that commit and the generator; it does not confer standards approval. External ontology imports are not fetched implicitly.
